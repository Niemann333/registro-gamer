His comment: I was trying to find on the internet the latest versions of the Jazz Jackrabbit 2 tunes (those that are packed inside the .j2b files), but every site gives you or the pre-release versions, or the versions brought by lori-central.com . The .j2b versions sounds different from the lori-central.com and pre-release versions so I decided to rip them.
Actually, there's no good .j2b ripper on internet
I found the only two ones:

-j2b2it.exe
This rips the content of .j2b files exporting them into .it files, but it will corrupt some of the pattern and instrument data giving you an almost correct playback of the tunes…

-Open ModPlug Tracker 1.18+
Opening the .j2b files with this one, you will obtain a perfect playback of the tunes, but you will lose ALL the unused data of the files. OpenMPT will delete: unused patterns, unused samples, unused samples/instruments text, original samples/instruments filenames…

You will not find on the internet a perfect algorithm that rips the content of .j2b files correctly.
So, how to rip the music data perfectly?
This is how I made it:
I dumped several times the content of my RAM into a single file without extension, while I was playing the game (I made a dumping of the RAM on every level with its specific tune).
Then I opened the dumped files with an hex editor trying to find the headers of .s3m and .it files.
When I found the beginnings and the endings of .s3m and .it files syntax, I copied all the text into new hex editor projects and then I exported them into new files.
The result was that I obtained a folder of 20 .s3m files and 5 .it files. ;)

In this folder there are ALL the tunes from Jazz Jackrabbit 2.
There are:
-the versions extracted from the .j2b files with original instrument/sample names;
-the game pre-release versions;
-additional tracks that are in the game folder;
-unused tracks;
