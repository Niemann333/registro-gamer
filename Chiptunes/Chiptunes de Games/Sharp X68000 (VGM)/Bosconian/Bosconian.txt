Notes:
The original 1981 arcade was developed by Namco
and distributed by Namco (J) and
Midway Games (U).
The X68000 version seems to be ported and
published by Dempa.

The songs were ripped from the sound test.
The music was composed specifically for this
port. The original arcade game only has a few
jingles, no real songs.
